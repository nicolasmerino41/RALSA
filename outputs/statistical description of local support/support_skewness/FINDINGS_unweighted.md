# Unweighted species occupancy to co-occurrence to support

All recorded species from both guilds receive equal weight. CO and support use the same known regional interacting pairs as the weighted analysis. No other input changes. Skewness is the third central moment divided by variance to the power 3/2, with no sample-size correction. Values are recalculated from the saved observations and verified against the existing summary.
